# 🏗️ BlackLogic Construction Studio

Welcome to **BlackLogic Construction Studio** — the first African digital construction platform that merges **civil engineering**, **road engineering**, and **software engineering** into one powerful ecosystem! 🚀

---

## 🎯 What is BlackLogic Construction Studio?

BlackLogic Construction Studio is a **digital studio** that empowers engineers, designers, and project managers to:

- Design buildings 🏢 and roads 🛣️ digitally.
- Generate AI-assisted prompts for **Project Management**, **Building Design**, and **Road Engineering** 🧠.
- Reduce 80% of traditional paperwork 📄❌.
- Collaborate seamlessly with teams inside one digital ecosystem 🤝.

It is designed for **desktop use** to provide maximum performance and precision. 💻

---

## 🛠️ How to Use

1. **Fill the Form Carefully** 📝  
   Provide the project type and details or goal.

2. **Generate AI Prompts** ⚡  
   Click **Generate Prompt** to create two actionable AI prompts.

3. **Copy Prompt** 🔗  
   Use the copy icon to copy the prompt to your clipboard.

4. **Ask Only Relevant Questions** 💡  
   AI prompts are specifically designed for:
   - Project Management
   - Building Design
   - Road Engineering

5. **Open AI Assistant** 🤖  
   Click the **Open AI** button to paste the prompt and get structured guidance.
